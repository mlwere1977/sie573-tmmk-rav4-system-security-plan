# Milestone 1: Team Charter (Week 2)

A living document setting out Team 5's goals, working agreement, and plan. It was finalized on 09/05/2026 and can be amended by unanimous vote.

> The signed charter PDF is not published because it contains team members' personal phone numbers and email addresses. This page summarizes its contents.

## Working agreement: organized around the three Scrum pillars

| Pillar | Expectations |
|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Ongoing collaboration through OneDrive, Teams, email, and text · professional, respectful communication · shared meeting minutes (Agenda / Discussion / Decisions / Action Items) |
| **Inspection** | Weekly synchronous meeting (Saturdays, Microsoft Teams) · punctuality, with work done ahead of review meetings |
| **Adaptation** | Advance notice and catch-up for missed meetings · charter reviewed at each milestone, amendments recorded in the change log |
